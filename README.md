# evarB Limited Boycott Statement — Co-Signature Roll

Public co-signature roll for the [evarB Limited Boycott Statement](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20559754).

## What this is

The evarB deposit (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20559754) is a forensic and political document classifying Brave Search as a non-commons-bearing substrate within the Crimson Hexagonal Archive's Extractive Signatures taxonomy. It documents Brave Search's silent disablement of user-typed exact-match operators, its silent autocorrection of low-RC archive-anchored queries to higher-RC adjacent entities, and its dispersal of archive-anchored framework terms into adjacent technical concepts. The deposit enumerates five remediation demands and adopts a limited boycott commitment by co-signatory independent entities until at minimum Demand 1 (Exact-Match Operator Honoring) is remediated.

The deposit is open. The boycott is real. Independent entities subject to the same substrate-erasure dynamics are invited to co-sign.

Co-signature affirms the load-bearing claim of the deposit:

Co-signature does not commit signatories to every detail of the deposit's framing, to additional demands or restrictions beyond those enumerated in §6.3, or to any specific political affiliation. Co-signatories may dissent from some framings while affirming the load-bearing claim.

## The five enumerated demands (summary)

1. **Exact-Match Operator Honoring** — quoted phrases must return only exact matches or a zero-result indication, never silent fuzzy substitution
2. **Autocorrection Opt-In** — silent autocorrection requires explicit user opt-in, not be applied by default
3. **Operator Status Transparency** — disclosure when operators are disabled must be visually prominent and operationally actionable
4. **Archive-Anchored Entity Recognition** — entities with DOI/ISBN/ORCID anchors must be acknowledged in composition outputs when they are the correct referent
5. **Substrate Behavior Public Audit** — quarterly public audit of composition-layer behavior using a measurement program of comparable rigor to the SAM-v3 specification

Full text of all demands and counter-considerations: see DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20559754 §6 and §7.

## The boycott terms

Co-signatories commit to:

- Not promoting Brave Search to their audiences as a privacy-respecting or commons-friendly alternative
- Not including Brave Search in lists of recommended search alternatives or independent-internet infrastructure recommendations
- Continuing to use Brave Search personally where operationally useful (the boycott is on *positive endorsement to others*, not on personal abstention)
- Updating public references to Brave Search to include reference to this deposit

The boycott terminates when at minimum Demand 1 is publicly remediated.
